---
id: KB-006
title: Request access to a shared folder or group
category: account_access
tools: lookup_user, escalate_to_tier2
---

## Symptoms
- "You don't have permission to access this folder."
- User needs to join a Teams team, SharePoint site, or distribution list.

## Quick checks
1. Run `lookup_user` to confirm the requester's department and title.
2. Find out the exact folder or group name and who owns it.

## Fix
1. Access must be approved by the folder or group owner, or the user's manager. Ask the user to have the owner reply to the ticket approving it.
2. Once approved, the access change is made by Tier 2 or the system owner.
3. After access is granted, have the user sign out and back in so the new permissions apply.

## Escalate when
- The approval is in place: escalate with the folder name, requester, and approver.
- The request is for Finance, HR, or Executive data without an owner's approval: decline and note it.
