title: External monitor or docking station not detected
category: devices_hardware

---

## Symptoms
- External monitor shows "No signal".
- Keyboard, mouse, or network through the dock stop working.

## Quick checks
1. Check every cable is firmly connected and the monitor is on the right input (HDMI vs DisplayPort).
2. Confirm the dock has power (light on).

## Fix
1. Unplug the dock from the laptop, wait 10 seconds, and plug it back in.
2. Press Windows + P and choose Extend.
3. Settings, System, Display, Detect.
4. Unplug the dock's power for 30 seconds to reset it.
5. Restart the laptop with the dock connected.
6. Test the monitor directly with a cable to the laptop to find out whether the dock or the monitor is at fault.

## Escalate when
- The dock or monitor is faulty and needs replacing.
