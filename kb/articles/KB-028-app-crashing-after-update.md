## Symptoms
- An app closes on its own or will not open after an update.
- "Not responding" right after launch.

## Quick checks
1. Ask which app, when it started, and whether colleagues on the same app have the same issue.
2. Run `check_device_status`; low disk space can break updates (KB-017).

## Fix
1. Restart the laptop to finish any pending update.
2. For Office apps: Settings, Apps, Microsoft 365, Modify, Quick Repair. If that fails, run Online Repair.
3. For other apps: uninstall and reinstall from the Company Portal.
4. Open the app's own update check and install the latest version, which may include a fix.

## Escalate when
- Several users are affected by the same update (Tier 2 may need to pause or roll back the update).
