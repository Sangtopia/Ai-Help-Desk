## Symptoms
- Wi-Fi icon shows connected but websites do not load.
- "No internet, secured" under the network name.

## Quick checks
1. Ask whether other devices on the same Wi-Fi work. If none do, it is the network, not the laptop.
2. In the office, ask whether colleagues nearby are affected.
3. Run `check_device_status`; an offline status confirms the laptop has no path to the internet.

## Fix
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on, or forget the network and reconnect.
2. Restart the laptop.
3. On home Wi-Fi, restart the router.
4. On hotel or guest Wi-Fi, open a browser to trigger the sign-in page.
5. Try a wired connection or phone hotspot to confirm the laptop itself works.

## Escalate when
- The office network is down for several people (P1 or P2 depending on how many are blocked).
