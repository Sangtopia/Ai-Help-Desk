id: KB-025
title: Teams audio, video, or screen sharing problems
category: software
tools: none
---

## Symptoms
- Others cannot hear the user, or the user cannot hear them.
- Camera shows black or "camera in use by another app".
- Screen share shows a black screen or the option is greyed out.

## Quick checks
1. In Teams, Settings, Devices: confirm the right microphone, speaker, and camera are selected (docks and headsets add extra devices).
2. Check the laptop's physical mute key and camera privacy shutter.
3. Close other apps that may hold the camera (Zoom, browser tabs with video calls).

## Fix
1. Use Teams' "Make a test call" to check audio.
2. Unplug and replug the headset or dock.
3. Fully quit Teams (right-click the taskbar icon, Quit) and reopen it.
4. Windows Settings, Privacy & security, Camera and Microphone: make sure Teams is allowed.
5. For screen sharing, share a single window instead of the whole screen, and restart Teams.

## Escalate when
- The problem happens on every call after a restart, or the camera is not detected by Windows at all (hardware fault).
